- Use the rear-wall assembly target R-value as the basis for insulation depth and attachment strategy.
- Seal sheathing transitions at balconies, decks, and roof interfaces before exterior insulation is installed.
- Review service penetrations so exhaust, intake, and hose-bib locations do not compromise the air barrier.
