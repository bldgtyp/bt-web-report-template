title: "Building Airtightness Notes"
dek: "Explain the target airtightness level and certification context."
---

Code-minimum construction in most states requires an airtightness rate of somewhere between 3 to 7 air-changes per hour (ACH), and the Colorado DFPC Energy Conservation Code 2021 / Zone 5(B) requires residential buildings in Climate Zones 3 to 8 to demonstrate an airtightness level of less than 5.0 ACH@50Pa.

In order to meet the recommended building performance level, this project should target an airtightness level tighter than standard code-minimum construction. Improving airtightness to this low level is one of the best ways to improve performance, increase comfort and durability, and reduce the risk of uncontrolled wintertime air leakage.
