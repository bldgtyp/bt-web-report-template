dek: "Show where added insulation still pays off and where area drives the loss."

The chart below shows how much heat each opaque assembly loses over the heating season as its R-value
changes. Each curve is one assembly at its modeled area; the markers show the R-value modeled in each
variant. The curves flatten as R-values rise, so the same added insulation saves less in an assembly
that is already well insulated. Large assemblies sit higher on the chart than small ones at the same
R-value.
