---
title: "Winter Radiation"
dek: "Review heating-season solar gains and the orientations that support winter performance."
---

Taking into account the climate, orientation, and shading, in the results below we have assessed the average seasonal (winter / summer) solar radiation falling upon the windows in the project. The radiation levels will vary by orientation and as can be observed, shading obstructions also have a strong effect on the final level of solar radiation any individual window receives.

- Very good solar radiation is observed along the south facade which will help to reduce the wintertime heating energy need significantly.
- Very limited solar gain observed on all east, west and north side glazing. However the relatively small area of windows on these facades mean that they do not significantly impact overall performance.
- While the high levels of solar gain on the south do help reduce heating energy demand, it is possible that at some times this solar gain may be unwanted. It is recommended to ensure that all south-facing glazing is outfitted with robust user-operable blinds / shades to ensure against any thermal discomfort. While external shades are preferred, internal shades are a good option as well and can still help with glare and overheating issues.
