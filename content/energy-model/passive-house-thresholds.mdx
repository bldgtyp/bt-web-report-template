The Passive House standard is controlled by a small set of annual demand and peak-load thresholds. Total annual energy consumption is useful as a high-level comparison, but certification depends on whether the selected variant stays within the applicable heating, cooling, and peak-load limits for the chosen path.

The checks below show the active model result against the corresponding Passive House target. Values above the dashed target line identify criteria that need design attention or a different certification path.
