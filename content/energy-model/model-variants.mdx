As shown in the results below, in order to assess the building performance we have tested the building in five distinct
configurations (variants):

1. **Code-Minimum:** A version which meets but does not exceed the **<VarLink
hrefKey="narrative.energy_code.code_base_url" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><Var
k="narrative.energy_code.name" /> / <Var k="narrative.energy_code.zone" /></VarLink>** code minimums for assembly
R-values, window U-values, airtightness and equipment efficiencies. This variant assumes a typical residential
"extract-only" (bath fan) ventilation system, and that the building uses all-electric appliances and equipment for
heating, cooling, hot-water and cooking.

2. **Improved Envelope:** This variant improves the window U-values, assembly R-values, and total airtightness of the
building beyond the "code-minimum" levels, but not quite to full "Passive House" level. This variant also includes a
dedicated fresh-air ventilation system with heat recovery (HRV). This system is assumed to be a "basic" unit with 75%
heat-recovery.

3. **Improved HVAC:** A variant which meets the stringent "Passive House Institute (PHI) Low Energy Building" standard
for envelope U-values, airtightness, windows, and equipment efficiencies. This variant also includes a high performance
ERV system for fresh-air ventilation. Note that due to the higher space heating allowance, lower gain (0.4 SHGC) glazing
is used in this variant which reduces the overall cooling load and improves summertime thermal comfort.

4. **PHI EnerPHit By Component [RECOMMENDED]:** A variant which meets the stringent "Passive House Institute (PHI)
Classic" standard for envelope U-values, airtightness, windows, and equipment efficiencies. This variant also includes a
high performance ERV system for fresh-air ventilation. Note that due to the lower space heating allowance, higher gain
(0.5 SHGC) glazing is used in this variant. As a result, i t is critical that all south, east and west facing glazing is
provided with durable solar control in order to reduce overheating risk in summer.

5. **PHI EnerPHit By Demand :** A version which matches, as best as can be determined at this time, the "as-drawn"
levels of insulation, airtightness. Note that this variant exceeds the Passive House targets, and so if desired some
small reduction in total insulation levels would be allowed while still meeting the certification targets. However there
is no issue with leaving insulation levels at the as-drawn level, if preferred.
